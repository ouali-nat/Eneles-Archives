# Quick start

Get up and running with Eneles in under two minutes. No installation required, everything runs in your browser.

## Create an account

Head to [/signup](/signup) and enter your name and email address. We email you a six-digit code to confirm the address. Enter it and you're in. There is no password: each time you sign in, you get a new code by email.

No credit card required. The free tier includes 20 image conversions and 40 PDF pages per month.

## Create your first project

From the [dashboard](/dashboard) or the [projects](/projects) page, click **New project**. The **Create project** dialog has four tabs:

- **New**: enter a **Project name** and an optional **Description**, then click **Create project**. The project starts with a `main.tex` holding a short article with a title and one section.
- **Templates**: browse starter documents by category (Notes, Paper, Beamer, Book, Homework, Resume, Letter, and Other) and click one. The dialog returns to the **New** tab, where you name the project and click **Create project**.
- **Import ZIP**: drop a `.zip` of an existing LaTeX project, or click to browse. The main `.tex` file is detected automatically. Click **Import project**.
- **ArXiv**: enter an arXiv ID, such as `2301.01234` or `2301.01234v2`, and click **Import from ArXiv** to import the paper's source files.

Templates that you and others have saved are on the [Templates](/templates) page; see [Editing](/docs/editing#templates).

## Write and compile

The editor gives you syntax highlighting, autocomplete, bracket matching, and live error hints. Your changes are saved automatically a few seconds after you stop typing.

To compile your document into a PDF, press `Ctrl + Enter` or click **Recompile** at the top of the PDF preview. The PDF appears in the right panel. If the compile fails, the errors appear there instead.

> **Tip:** Double-click anywhere in the PDF to jump to the line of source that produced it.
