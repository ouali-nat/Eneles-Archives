# Self-hosting

We're building first-class self-hosting support so universities and research institutions can run Eneles on their own infrastructure.

## What to expect

- **Docker Compose setup**: single command to spin up the full stack (frontend, API, database, TeX compiler).
- **Bring your own API keys**: for OpenAI, Anthropic, or local models for AI features.
- **LDAP / SAML integration**: for institutional single sign-on.
- **Full data ownership**: everything stays on your servers.

> **Note:** Self-hosting is on our 2026 roadmap. Follow the [changelog](/changelog) or star the [GitHub repo](https://github.com/Eneles-org) for updates.
